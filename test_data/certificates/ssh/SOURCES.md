## Category floor

## Private-key discipline

Generators produce SSH private keys as byproducts; those keys are
**not committed**. Add `ssh/private-keys/` (any such dir) to the
category-level `.gitignore` if your generator writes there. Cleaner:
have the generator `rm` the private key before exit.
